# Fractions — Chapter notes (rough)

Class 7 Maths. Chapter: Fractions — operations, comparisons, word problems.

Objectives: compare and order fractions; add and subtract fractions with unlike denominators; convert improper fractions to mixed numbers and back; solve word problems involving fractions.

Materials needed: fraction wall chart, cut-paper circles set (one per pair), chalkboard, NCERT textbook ch. exercise sheets, scissors and coloured paper for the activity, notebook.

Procedure — first revise what a fraction is: numerator, denominator, equivalent fractions from Class 6 quickly since they forget over summer. Then teach comparison using the fraction wall chart, show 2/3 > 3/5 visually before doing LCM method. Then addition with unlike denominators: find LCM of denominators, rewrite both fractions with common denominator, add numerators, simplify the result. Subtraction works exactly the same way except you subtract the numerators instead of adding them, so after addition the subtraction steps are identical: find LCM of denominators, rewrite both fractions with the common denominator, subtract the second numerator from the first, then simplify the resulting fraction if possible, e.g. 7/12 - 1/4 = 7/12 - 3/12 = 4/12 = 1/3.

Do the paper-circle activity in pairs: fold circles into halves, quarters, thirds and physically overlay pieces to verify sums before writing the arithmetic.

Improper to mixed: divide numerator by denominator, quotient is whole part, remainder stays over the denominator. Mixed to improper: multiply whole part by denominator and add numerator. Give 6 practice problems of each conversion.

Word problems: read twice, identify the wholes vs parts, decide the operation from the verb used (of means multiply, left means subtract), write the statement form before computing, and always simplify the final answer and state it with units where applicable.

Assessment: classwork exercise checked in the last ten minutes; exit slip with one comparison, one addition, one word problem; homework from textbook exercise; small quiz next Tuesday on operations.

Extra: remind students the fraction wall stays glued inside their notebook cover. Extra-extra: the paper circle sets need to be counted back in at the end of the period or they vanish; last year three red circles disappeared forever and the activity was short one set per row for the rest of term.
