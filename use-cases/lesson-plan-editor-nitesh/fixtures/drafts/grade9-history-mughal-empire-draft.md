# The Mughal Empire — intro lessons (raw notes)

babur 1526 panipat first battle vs ibrahim lodi — gunpowder + cavalry tactics
humayun — loses to sher shah suri, exile in persia, comes back briefly
akbar 1556 second battle of panipat vs hemu. expansion. rajput policy (marriage alliances + mansabdars). din-i-ilahi — mention but don't dwell
jahangir / nur jahan influence
